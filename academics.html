Academics at S.R.D. Public Convent

A UP Board-aligned curriculum structured around three clear stages — each one building steadily on the last.

Stage One · Nursery – LKG – UKG

Early Learning

Our pre-primary wing is warm and interactive by design. Children in Nursery, LKG and UKG learn through play, rhythm and repetition — building the cognitive and motor skills that make formal schooling feel natural rather than sudden.

  • •
    Cognitive DevelopmentShapes, colours, patterns and early reasoning through guided play.
  • •
    Motor Skill BuildingFine and gross motor activities woven into daily routines.
  • •
    Foundational Literacy & NumeracyLetters, sounds and numbers introduced through stories and songs.
Grade Levels

Nursery · LKG · UKG

Interactive, activity-based classrooms designed for a child's very first school experience — safe, warm and full of encouragement.

Play-based learning Story & song Warm classrooms
Stage Two · Classes 1 to 5

Primary Education

From Class 1, learning becomes more structured — but never rushed. We focus on solid concept-building in languages, mathematics and EVS, alongside early exposure to computers so children grow comfortable with technology from the start.

  • •
    LanguagesReading, writing and comprehension built step by step.
  • •
    MathematicsNumber sense and problem-solving through everyday examples.
  • •
    Environmental Studies (EVS)Curiosity about the world, science and surroundings.
  • •
    Early IT ExposureFormal computer education begins right here, in Class 1.

Classes 1 – 5

Stage Three · Classes 6 to 8

Middle School

Middle school students move into comprehensive, UP Board-aligned subject teaching across Science, English, Mathematics and Social Sciences — supported by focused clinics that strengthen exactly where each child needs it.

  • •
    ScienceConcept-driven teaching that connects theory to real observation.
  • •
    EnglishGrammar, comprehension and confident written expression.
  • •
    MathematicsStructured problem-solving building toward board-level standards.
  • •
    Social SciencesHistory, civics and geography taught with context and relevance.
