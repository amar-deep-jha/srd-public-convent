Academics

A concept-first UP Board curriculum from Nursery to Class 8 — where understanding comes before memorising, and every child is met at their own pace.

Our Approach

Learning, built on understanding

At S.R.D. Public Convent, we follow the UP Board curriculum with a teaching style that puts concepts first. Lessons are broken into small, clear steps; children are encouraged to ask questions; and every child gets the attention they need to truly understand before moving forward.

Our teachers plan lessons around what children can comfortably grasp, using everyday examples, classroom activities and frequent recall to make learning durable — not just testable.

  • UP Board Syllabus
  • Concept-first Lessons
  • Smart Classrooms
  • Computer Education from Class 1

Curriculum

What your child will study

A balanced day of languages, mathematics, science and creative learning — all aligned to the UP Board syllabus.

Languages

Hindi and English taught with a focus on reading with understanding, clear expression and confident handwriting from the earliest classes.

Mathematics

Numbers, shapes and problem-solving taught step by step, building the logic and confidence children carry into higher classes.

Science

Physics, chemistry and biology introduced through demonstrations, observations and experiments that make abstract ideas tangible.

Digital Skills

Computer education from Class 1, with practice in typing, basics of software, safe browsing and early programming concepts.

Social Science

History, geography and civics taught through stories, maps and current affairs that connect the classroom to the child's world.

Art & Sports

Daily time for creative expression and physical activity — drawing, craft, games and play that keep childhood joyful and balanced.

Extra Attention

Subject clinics & doubt hours

No child is left behind. Regular assessment helps teachers identify exactly where a student needs support, and structured subject clinics — extra, focused sessions — help them close those gaps at their own pace.
