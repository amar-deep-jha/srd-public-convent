Welcome to S.R.D. Public Convent

A nurturing foundation for every young learner

Located in Santha, Sant Kabir Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, S.R.D. Public Convent follows the UP Board curriculum with a warm, structured approach — from a child's very first day in Nursery through the growing independence of Class 8.

The Learning Path

Three stages, one steady climb

Our academic structure mirrors a child's real growth — from first steps in the classroom to confident subject mastery.

Stage One

Early Learning

Nursery · LKG · UKG

A warm, interactive pre-primary wing built around play, stories and structured routine.

  • Cognitive & motor skill development
  • Interactive, activity-based classrooms
  • Foundational language & number sense
Stage Two

Primary Education

Classes 1 to 5

Solid concept-building across core subjects, alongside early exposure to computers.

  • Languages, Mathematics & EVS
  • Computer education from Class 1
  • Regular assessment & feedback
Stage Three

Middle School

Classes 6 to 8

Comprehensive subject clinics aligned closely with UP Board standards.

  • Science, English, Maths & Social Science
  • Focused English & Science clinics
  • Exam-readiness & study skills
