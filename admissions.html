How It Works

Our Admission Process

A simple, four-step path from enquiry to enrolment — our admissions desk will guide you at every stage.

Submit an Enquiry

Fill out the admission enquiry form with your child's details, or call our helpdesk directly.

Campus Visit & Interaction

Visit the campus to meet our teachers, see the classrooms, and get your questions answered.

Document Verification

Submit the required documents — birth certificate, previous school records (if any), and address proof.
