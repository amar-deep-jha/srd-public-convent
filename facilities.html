Campus Facilities

Everything on our campus is built to support real, everyday learning — for children in Nursery through Class 8.

Computer Education

Formal digital literacy begins right from Class 1, giving students an early, confident start with technology.

Smart Classrooms

Audio-visual, technology-supported classrooms that turn every lesson into an interactive experience.

Reference Library

A dedicated reading room with reference material to help students explore beyond the textbook.

Focused Subject Clinics

Specialised support sessions in English & Science fundamentals for students who need extra reinforcement.

Small Class Sizes

A high teacher-to-student attention ratio, so every child gets to be seen, heard and encouraged.

Health & Wellness

Regular on-campus medical check-ups to help keep every student healthy through the school year.
