Our Facilities

Everything a growing child needs — classrooms, technology, books, science, sports, transport and care — all on one campus in Santha.

Campus & Infrastructure

Built for learning, play and safety

Seven campus facilities that work together to support academic, physical and emotional growth.

Smart Classrooms

Every lesson can come alive with digital aids — making science, mathematics and languages vivid and easy to grasp.

Reference Library

A quiet corner stocked with textbooks, reference books and storybooks that encourages reading beyond the syllabus.

Computer Education

Dedicated computer training from Class 1 — typing, fundamentals, safe browsing and early programming habits.

Science Learning

Demonstrations and hands-on experiments that turn abstract principles into memorable, visible experience.

Sports & Play Ground

A dedicated open ground for games and daily physical activity — because healthy bodies build healthy minds.

School Transport

Reliable transport that brings children from the surrounding villages to campus and back home, safely.

Health & Wellness

Hygiene routines, regular check-ins and a caring eye on every child's wellbeing, throughout the school day.

Event & Cultural Days

Festivals, celebrations and cultural programmes where children perform, present and grow in confidence.

A Supportive, Safe Campus

A secure, supervised environment where every adult on campus is responsible for your child's wellbeing.
