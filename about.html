About S.R.D. Public Convent

A UP Board institution in Santha, Sant Kabir Nagar, built around one belief: every child deserves a nurturing place to learn, grow and shine.

Our Identity

Rooted in Santha, built for young minds

S.R.D. Public Convent serves the community of Santha, Sant Kabir Nagar, Uttar Pradesh, offering education from Nursery through Class 8 under the UP Board curriculum. We believe a school's first responsibility is to make a child feel safe, seen and genuinely curious about the world.

Our classrooms stay intentionally small, our teachers stay closely involved, and our campus is designed so that every stage of a child's early education — from first alphabets to board-aligned subject clinics — happens under one caring roof.

What Guides Us

Our Mission & Vision

Two commitments shape everything we do — what we want to achieve for your child today, and the school we want to be tomorrow.

Our Mission

To provide every child with concept-first, personally attentive education — building strong foundations in language, mathematics, science and digital literacy from the earliest years, so that learning always feels achievable and encouraging.

Our Vision

To be Santha's most trusted school for early and middle education — a place where families know their children are nurtured academically, emotionally and physically, all the way from Nursery to Class 8.

School Philosophy

How we teach

We do not rush childhood. Concepts are introduced step by step, questions are welcomed, and mistakes are treated as part of learning. Our teachers build genuine understanding first — confidence, then speed, follows naturally.

Every stage of a child's growth — cognitive, emotional and physical — is tended to on the same campus, by the same caring team, from the first day of Nursery to the final year of Class 8.
